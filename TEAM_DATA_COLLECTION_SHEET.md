## Tips for Great Submissions

### Photos
- ✅ Well-lit, professional but approachable
- ✅ Solid background or blurred
- ✅ Professional attire (business casual minimum)
- ❌ Avoid selfies, group photos, or party pictures

### Project Descriptions
- ✅ Focus on **impact and results**, not just technologies used
- ✅ Use specific numbers (75% faster, $120K saved, 99.9% uptime)
- ✅ Tell a story: problem → solution → results
- ❌ Avoid jargon-heavy descriptions that clients won't understand

### Bio Writing
- ✅ First person ("I discovered...") feels more personal
- ✅ Show personality - you're not a robot
- ✅ Balance technical credibility with approachability
- ❌ Don't just list resume items - tell your story

### Testimonials
- ✅ Specific praise is better than generic ("saved us 200 hours" vs "great to work with")
- ✅ Include context about the project
- ✅ Real names and titles add credibility
- ❌ Don't fake testimonials - authenticity matters
